RACKS

10 cups, start Pyramid 4-3-2-1 at the table end.
6 cups, re-rack 3-2-1, rebuilt from the tip.
3 cups, re-rack 2-1, rebuilt from the tip.
1 cup, re-rack Last cup moves to the tip.

TURN BANNERS

YOUR TURN You hold both balls.
THEIR TURN Opponent throws both balls.
BALLS BACK Both balls hit. Throw again, once per turn.
RE-RACK 6, 3 or 1 cups left after a turn.
BOUNCE, 2 CUPS Bounce shot lands. Thrower picks the second cup.
RE-ROLL First thrower clears the rack. The other side gets a last turn.
FOUL, CUP LOST Ball or table touched out of turn, or own cup knocked over.
